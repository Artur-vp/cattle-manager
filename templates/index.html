Cattle Manager

A simple and organized way to manage your herd

New animal

Add animals with ID, birth date and value

Catalog

View age, value, sex and herd charts

History

Track every removal with reason and date

After creating an account and logging in, you can register each animal with its identification, date of birth, and value. The system automatically calculates the age in years and months, keeping information always up to date.

The catalog page displays your complete herd list alongside a chart showing the distribution between males and females, giving you a quick overview of your herd composition.

When an animal leaves the herd, register the reason — sale, death, or transfer — and keep a permanent removal log of every animal that has passed through your farm.

Replace paper records and spreadsheets with a fast, accessible, and secure digital tool — available from any device with a browser.
